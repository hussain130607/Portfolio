Our story

A little shop with a big heart

Gift R began as a tiny shop in Kandirpar, Cumilla with a single display table, a roll of pink ribbon and one simple idea: every gift should feel personal. Our founder, Rumana, spent her evenings hand-wrapping presents for neighbours and friends — and word quickly spread.

Today, we're a passionate team of curators, makers and wrapping artists who handpick every item in our collection. We partner with independent artisans and small bakeries to bring you gifts with genuine character, then wrap each one as if it were for our own family.

Whether it's a first birthday, a golden anniversary or a "just because" moment, we're honoured to be part of your celebrations.

— Rumana, Founder of Gift R

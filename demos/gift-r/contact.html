Quick answers

Frequently Asked Questions

✦
How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery takes 2–4 working days and is free on orders over $75.00. Express (next-day) delivery is available for $12.99.

Can I send a gift directly to someone else?

Absolutely! At checkout, tick "This order is a gift" and enter the recipient's details. Prices are never included in the package.

What is your return policy?

Unused, non-personalized items can be returned within 30 days for a full refund. Personalized gifts are made to order and can only be returned if faulty.

Do you offer corporate or bulk gifting?

Yes — we create custom branded gift boxes for teams and clients. Send us a message with the subject "Corporate gifting" and we'll be in touch within 24 hours.
